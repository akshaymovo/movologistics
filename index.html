About MOVO LOGISTICS

MOVO LOGISTICS is a modern and rapidly growing logistics operations company established in 2025 by Malkhan Sharma with a vision to build a reliable, efficient, and professionally managed hyperlocal delivery network across India. The company was founded with the objective of delivering scalable operational solutions that meet the evolving demands of the logistics and last-mile delivery industry.

Details

Currently, MOVO LOGISTICS specializes in 2W (Two-Wheeler) delivery operations and works in collaboration with multiple logistics and commerce platforms to support their day-to-day operational requirements. The company focuses on rider management, onboarding, operational coordination, shift handling, and maintaining high service standards to ensure smooth and disciplined delivery operations.
