Welcome to MOVO LOGISTICS

MOVO LOGISTICS is a modern and rapidly growing logistics operations company established in 2025 by Malkhan Sharma, with a clear vision to build a reliable, efficient, scalable, and professionally managed hyperlocal delivery network across India.

In today's rapidly evolving logistics and e-commerce ecosystem, timely delivery, reliable delivery partners, operational discipline, and strong ground-level coordination play a critical role in business success.

At MOVO LOGISTICS, we understand these operational requirements and work toward creating structured solutions that help logistics and commerce platforms manage their last-mile delivery operations efficiently.

Currently, MOVO LOGISTICS specializes in 2W (Two-Wheeler) delivery operations and works in collaboration with logistics and commerce platforms to support their day-to-day operational requirements.

Our focus includes rider management, delivery partner onboarding, operational coordination, shift handling, ground-level operations, and maintaining high service standards to ensure smooth and disciplined delivery operations.
